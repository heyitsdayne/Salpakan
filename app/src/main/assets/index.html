Game of the Generals · you are blue, at the bottom

How to play

Move one piece one square up, down, left or right. Move onto an enemy piece to challenge it. The higher rank wins and the loser is removed; equal ranks both go. You never see enemy ranks.

Ranks, strongest first. The Spy beats every officer but loses to the Private. Anything that challenges the Flag wins.

Win by capturing the enemy Flag or by walking your Flag to the far row. Before starting, tap two of your pieces to swap them.
